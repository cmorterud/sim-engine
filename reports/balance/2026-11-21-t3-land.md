Role choices made while solving, against the counter matrix:

* The Heavy Walker is the tier's line unit at three Heavy Tanks' cost, half again the anchor's range, and fights longer (time to kill 1.3 times the anchor's, for `fight_seconds_t3`). It keeps its personal shield of 700, which the duels that price it count, and a gatling's many small shots, so that no one shot decides a kill.
* The Siege Walker is heavy: slow (0.6 of the anchor's speed), with 2.2 times the anchor's time to kill and one big cannon reaching a tenth past the Heavy Walker's gun, priced even with it. It beats the artillery even attack-moving: the counter matrix makes that a micro cell for line, assault, and raider units, and a heavy unit walks through artillery fire.
* Mobile Heavy Artillery is for sieges first. It reaches 1,680, a fifth past the T3 Heavy Point Defense's 1,400 (`[siege] outrange_defense_min`), so that it shells defenses without reply, with a least range of a quarter of its reach, 420, inside the Heavy Walker's 525, so that what closes on it can fight it. It is priced as T1 artillery is, to beat line units that attack-move into it, keeping about half; micro flips that. An equal materiel of it, 16, breaks a heavy shield over three heavy point defenses in 27 seconds and loses nothing (`[siege] seconds_max`). Its splash reaches about one formation spacing of a T3 target, where the starting value of 80 was four times that. Raiders, and Medium Tanks that crowd inside its least range, beat it; Assault Hovercraft, with 1,260 units to cross under its fire, no longer do.
* Two first tries are kept as lessons. Priced by the field duel alone, with its least range half its reach, it was cut to 945, below even the T2 point defense's 1,000: the walkers fired from inside its least range, so it had to be short to be hit at all. Priced instead by a siege time of 60 seconds, it came out at 0.15 strength, 1,026 health and 64 damage a second, since a position it outranges cannot fight back and any strength breaks it in time; at that it lost to everything in the field. The siege time is now a check, not its price.

Not in this pass: the Field Rig and the T3 Engineer, builders, for the economy knobs.
