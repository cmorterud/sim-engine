Role choices made while solving, against the counter matrix:

* The Heavy Walker is the tier's line unit at three Heavy Tanks' cost, half again the anchor's range, and fights longer (time to kill 1.3 times the anchor's, for `fight_seconds_t3`). It keeps its personal shield of 700, which the duels that price it count, and a gatling's many small shots, so that no one shot decides a kill.
* The Siege Walker is heavy: slow (0.6 of the anchor's speed), with 2.2 times the anchor's time to kill and one big cannon reaching a tenth past the Heavy Walker's gun, priced even with it. It beats the artillery even attack-moving: the counter matrix makes that a micro cell for line, assault, and raider units, and a heavy unit walks through artillery fire.
* Mobile Heavy Artillery is artillery as at T1, 1.8 times the line unit's range. At 2.4 times, its least range, half its reach, fell beyond the Heavy Walker's range, so the walkers fired from where it could never hit them, and pricing ran its strength to the solver's limit of eight times. As at T1, line units fire from just inside its least range. Its splash reaches about one formation spacing of a T3 target, where the starting value of 80 was four times that. Medium Tanks crowd inside its least range and beat it outright, which keeps T1 useful against it.

Not in this pass: the Field Rig and the T3 Engineer, builders, for the economy knobs.
