Each role's strength multiplier is solved for one measured target: the Heavy Tank for its strength per materiel against the anchor, by break-even; the rest for a mirrored duel score against what they are meant to beat or match. Duels are at 1,440 materiel a side, averaged over four starting layouts, both ways round. Scores are the first side's share of materiel left, weighted by health, less the second's. The derivation takes about 25 seconds.

Role choices made while solving, against the counter matrix:

* The Heavy Tank is the tier's line unit at three anchors' cost (`[tiers] cost_multiplier` 2.5–3.5), a quarter more range, and fights a little longer (time to kill 1.15 times the anchor's, for `fight_seconds_t2`). It is priced to the tier efficiency, 1.07 times the anchor per materiel, not by a duel score, so that it wins by range and toughness rather than by being a better buy. It still loses to Light Artillery attack-moving, as T1 line does.
* The Grenadier Walker is a splash skirmisher: its range is a fifth past the Heavy Tank's (`skirmisher_range_edge_min`), at its speed, with splash reaching about one formation spacing. It beats the Heavy Tank attack-moving and loses to it spread out and charging: a micro cell, as T1 line against artillery is. Its range is held to the minimum edge so that Light Artillery outranges it and comes out about even. Its splash takes clumps of small T1 units apart, raiders included, even spread out: the cost of a splash role, which BALANCE.md accepts for artillery too.
* The Assault Hovercraft was first priced to beat the grenadier, and then beat everything, the Heavy Tank included. It is now about even with the Heavy Tank, a little under for crossing water, and fast (the anchor's speed) so that it closes on artillery and grenadiers rather than being kited: fast units that close in are among artillery's counters. A reload of 1.6 seconds keeps its shot away from the strength where one more hit is needed to kill a Heavy Tank: at 1.5 the score jumped from −0.20 to +0.08 between two strengths 1% apart.
* The Mobile Missile Launcher is siege, priced to beat Light Artillery firing in place, since artillery counters artillery. Its cruise missiles neither lead nor follow, and its splash is incidental: with a splash role's, tanks marching in column walked into where each missile was aimed, and it beat the Heavy Tank. Now what keeps moving beats it, and a few raiders take it apart.
* A score target can land on a step: a shot just becoming enough to kill in one fewer hit moves the score by a third. Pricing now keeps the strength whose measured score came nearest the target, not the middle of the last bisection step, which sat past such a step.

Not in this pass: the Mobile Flak (it hits only aircraft, for the air pass), the Mobile Shield (it needs mixed armies in the harness), and the T2 Engineer and Combat Engineer (builders, for the economy knobs).
