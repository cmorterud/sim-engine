# T1 navy, 2026-11-24

The first naval pass of `[domains]` (BALANCE.md): the Frigate, the Attack Submarine, and the T1
Torpedo Launcher, the coast's answer to them, against T1 land and air frozen.
`--balance-derive domains` prints the tables below; `tests/balance_test.rs` checks them.

## How the numbers were set

Each was half as strong per cost against the anchor as the inspiration has its own against its T1
tank (`2026-11-24-inspiration-ratios.md`): costed about 1.35 times as dear against the Medium Tank
as the inspiration's against its tank, with about 0.7 times the health and 0.75 to 0.8 times the
damage. Their ranges and speeds already matched. So each takes the inspiration's shape against its
own T1 tank, applied to the Medium Tank: cost (mass and energy together), health, and damage, each
the median across the inspiration's factions of its ratio to that faction's T1 tank, and build time
at the inspiration's build time per mass against its tank's. Ratios, never values.

Two numbers then went past those ratios, within `[domains] strength_band`, for cells the ratios
alone did not meet:

* **The frigate's railgun at aircraft**, 13.9 a second by the ratio, is 20.8, half again, so that
  frigates hold their own against an equal cost of T1 bombers over water
  (`frigates_against_t1_bombers_at_least`, -0.15). At 13.9 they lost at -0.48, at 18.0 -0.18, at
  20.8 -0.02, and at 27.8 they won at +0.40. Navy is strong at sea ([domains]); the T1 frigate's
  anti-air is weak in the inspiration, and this lifts it only to even.
* **The T1 torpedo launcher's health**, 2,482 by the ratio, is 3,000, so that an equal cost of
  frigates closing on three of them loses (`frigates_break_t1_torpedo_launchers = false`). At 2,482
  four frigates broke them in 19 s with a fifth of themselves left; at 3,000 they lose every ship.
  Its strength per cost is then 1.07 times the inspiration's ratio.

Navy already wins where only ships and hovercraft can reach the enemy: against Air, 36 of 50 on
Scattered Isles, 46 on 20 km Islands maps with lots of water, and all 50 on 10 km ones. Wherever
land and air can also reach the enemy, Air crushes Naval: 3 of 50 for Naval on Twin Passes, none on
Stillwater, 4 and 11 on Central Lake maps. That is the harness's finding in whole matches: a fleet
loses to an equal cost of aircraft. The map targets of `[domains]` ask for Naval to be a viable
choice, 35% to 65% against each strategy, on those mixed maps.
