# T3 air and navy, 2026-11-24

The third naval pass of `[domains]` (BALANCE.md), and T3 air with it, against T1 and T2 frozen:
the Battleship, Battlecruiser, and Heavy Gunship, and what keeps a massed T3 fleet safe from the
air. `--balance-derive domains` prints the tables below; `tests/balance_test.rs` checks them.

## How the numbers were set

By the inspiration's ratios against its own T1 tank, applied to the Medium Tank, as at T1 and T2
(`2026-11-24-t1-navy.md`, `2026-11-24-t2-air-navy.md`), keeping the damage already decided:

* **The Heavy Gunship** takes its ratios whole: cost, build time, health, and damage.
* **The Battleship** takes its ratio's cost and build time. Its turrets keep the damage set
  against T3 artillery, so health carries the rest: 107,000, half again its ratio's 72,450, which
  puts it at 0.89 of the inspiration's strength per cost, within the band.
* **The Battlecruiser** takes its ratio's cost, build time, and health, and its lasers keep twice a
  turret's damage, which puts it at 1.23, at the top of the band.
* **The Strategic Bomber** is already at the inspiration's ratio (0.23 of the anchor's strength per
  cost against its 0.23), and keeps the numbers `[kills]` set it by.

## A massed T3 fleet and the air

The first target, torpedo bombers needing three times a massed T3 fleet's cost to break it, could
not be met by the escorts of the mix then measured (one of each ship, as the AI built): its
battleship and battlecruiser were three-quarters of its cost, and add health but no anti-air, so
they dilute the fleet's defense per cost. Giving the capital ships bursting anti-air, and
anti-torpedo charges of up to ten a second, reached 1.9 times at best, with more cruisers, and not
the target.

The inspiration does neither. Its battleships, in all four factions, have about 1% of a cruiser's
anti-air per cost and no anti-torpedo charge, only missile defenses; its one battlecruiser has a
charge every 10 seconds, as ours does. Its fleets are kept from the air by their escorts: the
cruisers' anti-air and the destroyers' charges. So, decided 2026-11-24: the escorts carry it, the
capital ships keep the inspiration's shape, and a massed T3 fleet, escorts two-thirds of its cost,
needs at least twice its cost in torpedo bombers to break, as a T2 fleet does
(`torpedo_bombers_to_break_fleet_t3`, from 3 times). A battleship-heavy fleet without its escorts
stays torpedo bombers' prey, the counterplay to skimping on them
(`battleship_heavy_fleet_breaks_to_torpedo_bombers_at_most`, 1).

19-ship order that starts with the battleship. Until today they built one of each, a
battleship-heavy fleet that loses to an equal cost of the T3 air force. At twice the force, the
one-of-each fleet comes out ahead of the T3 air force (+0.37), but torpedo bombers still break it
at 0.35 times its cost.

## Coastal sieges at an equal cost

Whole ships buy only near a position's cost: one battleship is 0.70 of a coastal T3 position's.
A siege's length goes about as the inverse of the guns on it, since the ships shell from beyond
the defenses' reach and lose nothing, so the report now puts its time at an equal cost by that
ratio. The battleship's 163 seconds by 0.70 of the cost is about 114 at an equal cost, within
`coastal_seconds_max` (120). The cruisers' 91 seconds by 0.67 of the cost is about 61.
