# T2 air and navy, 2026-11-24

> Correction, 2026-11-24: its fights between ships and aircraft over open sea were measured while aircraft there ignored their orders and circled where they started, which favored ships. They are measured again, and the numbers set from them changed, in `2026-11-24-t3-air-navy.md`, Aircraft over open sea.

The second naval pass of `[domains]` (BALANCE.md), and T2 air with it, against T1 frozen: the
Destroyer, Cruiser, Torpedo Boat, Escort Corvette, and T2 Torpedo Launcher; the Gunship, Torpedo
Bomber, and Fighter-Bomber; and the Mobile Flak, T2 air's answer on land.
`--balance-derive domains` prints the tables below; `tests/balance_test.rs` checks them.

## How the numbers were set

First, the inspiration's ratios, as for T1 navy (`2026-11-24-t1-navy.md`): each unit takes the
inspiration's cost, health, damage, and build time per mass against its own T1 tank, applied to
the Medium Tank. That put every one within `[domains] strength_band`, and left the fights between
domains where they were, since it raised navy and T2 air together: the T2 fleet still lost to an
equal cost of the AI's air force at -0.62, and torpedo bombers still broke it at 0.65 times its
cost. The Escort Corvette was already within its ratio (its shield's health per cost 1.73 against
the inspiration's 1.85) and kept its numbers.

Then what keeps a fleet alive in play, which the static ratios leave out (`[domains]`):

* **Anti-air that bursts over massed aircraft.** The cruiser's SAMs had a blast of 6, killing one
  aircraft at a time. With the mobile flak's blast of 80, the fleet beats an equal cost of air:

  | SAM blast | T2 fleet against the T2 air force | Torpedo bombers to break the fleet |
  |---|---|---|
  | 6 | -0.62 | 0.65 times its cost |
  | 40 | +0.35 | 1.20 |
  | 80 | +0.65 | 2.03 |

* **Anti-torpedo charges that keep up.** The corvette's stops one torpedo every 3 seconds, from 6,
  which takes the torpedo bombers needed from 2.03 to 2.24 times the fleet's cost, a margin over
  the target of 2. The destroyer's stops one a second, from one every 10, and its torpedoes do six
  times its ratio's, 560: it is the fleet's hunter of submarines, and beats an equal cost of them at
  +0.60, where it lost at -0.81. Destroyers alone still lose to torpedo bombers (-0.98): a wing of
  them launches far more torpedoes than two charges stop.
* **Torpedo launchers that still answer ships.** With the destroyer's charge, it shot down every
  torpedo three T2 launchers fired as one-torpedo volleys, and broke them without loss. The
  launcher now fires its four torpedoes a volley each on its own, at the same damage a second, so
  that the charge must stop every one, and has 12,000 health, from 8,991 by its ratio, within the
  band: an equal cost of destroyers closing on it loses every ship.
* **The cruiser's cruise missiles** are at the top of the band (1.19 of the ratio), so that an equal
  cost of cruisers breaks a coastal position of T2 point defenses under a dome in 91 seconds,
  losing nothing. Coastal sieges have their own limit, 120 seconds (`coastal_seconds_max`), past
  artillery's 60: artillery takes fire while it shells, and ships shell from beyond the defenses'
  reach, so their pace is all that is left to set.
* **The Mobile Flak** is set by the anti-air share rule at T2, as the T1 mobile anti-air is at T1, not
  by a ratio: a T2 land army a fifth flak by materiel comes out even with an equal cost of the AI's
  T2 air force. Its shell went from 72 to 64 for that.
