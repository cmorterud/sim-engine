Strength multipliers are solved by mirrored duels against the anchor, averaged over four starting layouts, at 480 materiel a side. Role choices made while solving, against the counter matrix:

* Artillery's least range is half its range, and raiders' range 0.6 of the anchor's, so that raiders work inside it.
* Splash is about one formation spacing of reach (splash plus the target's radius): at twice the spacing, one shell hit a three by three block of raiders.
* Massed raiders against massed artillery is a micro cell, like line units against artillery; raiders beat artillery without micro in a raid, a few against artillery left alone.
* Micro is measured as a flip of the equal-materiel score: as an efficiency ratio it ran to thousands of percent.
