sim-engine

A real-time strategy game of factories, engineers, and armies of hundreds. Build an economy, out-produce your opponent, and destroy their commander to win.

Watch an AI match

Two AIs play each other, the same match the game's soak test plays for a map and seed, so a seed always plays out the same way. See everything, or only what one side sees, as if playing as them.

The soak plays seeds 1–200 on Skirmish, 1–120 on the others.
In the match: V switches whose sight, - and = change the speed up to 16x, and Space pauses.

Arrow keys

Letter keys
The key in the corner of each button. Hover over a button for what it does
